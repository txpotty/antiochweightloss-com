    How Sermorelin Therapy Works in San Ramon, CA

    How Sermorelin Works in San Ramon

    Sermorelin is a 29 amino acid fragment of growth hormone releasing hormone. After injection it attaches to the receptors your own releasing hormone uses, which tells the pituitary to send out growth hormone in normal nightly pulses. Growth hormone then raises IGF-1. Because somatostatin and other feedback brakes stay active, levels usually climb more gently than with direct growth hormone shots. The original brand, Geref, was discontinued in 2008, and today's sermorelin is compounded.

    Who Sermorelin Is Usually For

    Typical candidates are adults over 35 who notice poorer sleep, slower recovery or a softer middle despite steady habits. A low-normal IGF-1 for your age can make sermorelin easier to justify and easier to monitor. It is not for weight loss alone. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, active cancer or a known pituitary tumor rules it out.

    Sermorelin Dosing and Schedule in San Ramon

    Bedtime is the usual slot. A common starting range is 200 to 300 micrograms injected under the skin each night, often five nights on and two off, with your provider choosing the final dose. Injecting on an empty stomach, about two hours after your last meal, may help. Most shipments are a freeze-dried powder that you reconstitute with bacteriostatic water and then refrigerate.

    What the First Three Months Look Like

    Weeks one and two are mostly about the routine. Deeper sleep is the earliest change some patients report, though many feel no difference yet. Between the first and third month, some adults describe better recovery after exercise, while changes in fat and muscle tend to lag behind and depend on diet. Your provider often rechecks IGF-1 around week six to eight.

    Sermorelin Side Effects in San Ramon

    Redness, itching or a small bump where you inject is the most common complaint. Flushing, headache, dizziness and mild nausea also show up, mostly in the first few weeks. A smaller group notices restlessness, poor sleep or an odd taste, while hives, throat swelling or wheezing mean you should seek emergency care right away. Tell your provider at the next check-in.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Before any prescription, your provider reads the intake for cancer history, pituitary problems, thyroid disease, diabetes, sleep apnea and pregnancy plans. Baseline labs often include IGF-1, a metabolic panel and TSH. Low thyroid that has not been treated, or regular steroid use like prednisone, can weaken the effect, which is why the medication list matters. Lab results can be uploaded.

    Sermorelin Compared to Other Peptides in San Ramon

    People ask about CJC-1295 most. The CJC-1295 and ipamorelin combination works on the same axis with a longer action and a second receptor target. Tesamorelin is a stabilized cousin of sermorelin that is FDA-approved as Egrifta SV for abdominal fat in adults with HIV lipodystrophy. Actual growth hormone, by contrast, is a different drug for adults with confirmed deficiency.
