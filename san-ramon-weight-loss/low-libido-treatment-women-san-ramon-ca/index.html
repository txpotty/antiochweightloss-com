    What Causes Low Libido in San Ramon

    Low libido in women rarely has a single cause. Common contributors include SSRIs and SNRIs, hormonal birth control, perimenopause, thyroid problems, chronic stress, poor sleep, pain during sex and relationship strain. So does how you feel in your body. When low desire is persistent, distressing and not better explained by another cause, it may be diagnosed as hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. That label opens up FDA-approved options.

    Who Low Libido Treatment Is For

    This is for adult women bothered by a drop in desire or arousal. It often helps women whose interest changed after starting a medication, having a baby, entering perimenopause or going through a stressful stretch at work or home. You can start without a label. Women who are simply not bothered by lower desire do not need treatment, since HSDD is defined partly by distress. Your provider helps sort it out.

    Treatment Options and Schedules in San Ramon

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is a 100 mg tablet taken every night at bedtime. Bremelanotide, sold as Vyleesi and also called PT-141, is a 1.75 mg injection under the skin used at least 45 minutes before sex, no more than once a day or eight times a month. Oxytocin is compounded. It comes as a nasal spray or troche before intimacy, used off-label and without FDA approval. Some women need no medication at all.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly review. Early steps often include adjusting a medication, treating dryness or improving sleep, sometimes alongside a new prescription. PT-141 effects show up within hours. Flibanserin builds over four to eight weeks, and providers usually stop it at eight weeks if nothing has improved. Check-ins track the change.

    Side Effects in San Ramon

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, fatigue and low blood pressure. Alcohol and certain interacting drugs make them stronger, which is why it is taken at bedtime with drinking limits. PT-141 often causes nausea. Flushing, headache, injection site redness, a short blood pressure bump and skin darkening with frequent use are also possible. Oxytocin can cause headache.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Liver disease rules out flibanserin. Drugs like fluconazole, clarithromycin and certain HIV medications rule it out, and drinking has to be kept away from the bedtime dose. Blood pressure matters for PT-141. Women with high blood pressure that is not controlled, or with heart disease, should not use PT-141. Neither is used during pregnancy.

    Comparing Low Libido Options in San Ramon

    Flibanserin is daily and steady. It tends to suit women who rarely drink and prefer one routine pill, though the average benefit in trials was modest, around one extra satisfying sexual event per month compared with placebo. PT-141 is taken only when wanted. That suits some women, but nausea puts others off. Oxytocin is gentler, with limited research.
