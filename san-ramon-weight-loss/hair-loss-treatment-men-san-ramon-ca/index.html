    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in San Ramon, CA

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in San Ramon

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. In genetically sensitive men, DHT gradually miniaturizes follicles, starting at the temples or crown and moving inward. Finasteride lowers DHT, which slows or stops that shrinking. Minoxidil works on a separate track, lengthening the growth phase.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. It works best when there is still visible hair in the thinning areas, because follicles that have been bald for years rarely respond to any medicine. Earlier is better. Sudden, patchy or painful hair loss needs an in-person exam instead. The provider checks your photos for the typical pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in San Ramon

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Minoxidil comes as a topical used once or twice a day, or as a low-dose off-label tablet. Compounded sprays that combine finasteride and minoxidil go on once daily. Dutasteride, when used, is a daily capsule.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. The early shed is usually a sign follicles are cycling, and it fades. By month six, loss typically slows and density starts to improve. Expect the full effect near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in San Ramon

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. Most sexual side effects go away after stopping, and some fade on their own. Mood changes are rare but should be reported promptly. Topical minoxidil can dry or irritate the scalp. Oral minoxidil can cause extra body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. They also ask directly about past depression, current sexual health and any plans to have children soon, since those shape which medicine is chosen. Finasteride lowers PSA results by about half. Tell any doctor who checks your prostate that you take it.

    How Men's Options Compare in San Ramon

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. When finasteride is not enough, providers may move to dutasteride, which is off-label for hair. Men avoiding DHT blockers can use a compounded minoxidil and GHK-Cu topical. Transplants help some men.
